About GroupSpace

GroupSpace is a collaborative workspace designed to help students, organizations, and project teams organize their work and communicate more efficiently.

🎯

Our Purpose

GroupSpace brings discussions, tasks, files, schedules, expenses, and team members into one organized platform.

👥

Built for Teams

Create multiple workspaces for school projects, organizations, events, businesses, or personal groups.

⚡

Simple Collaboration

Our goal is to make teamwork easier by reducing the need to switch between multiple applications.

Organized Workspaces

Each workspace keeps its own discussions, files, tasks, calendar, expenses, and members organized separately.
