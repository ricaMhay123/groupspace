# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Students, project teams, hackathon teammates, and student organizations collaborating on group projects, coursework, and shared activities.

## Product Purpose
GroupSpace is an all-in-one collaborative workspace that unites task tracking (Kanban), calendar scheduling, MS Word-style notebook authoring with vector flowcharts and digital inking, shared expense auditing, and isolated personal spaces for private productivity.

## Positioning
Unlike fragmented tools or overly complex enterprise suites, GroupSpace offers instantaneous, zero-overhead group orchestration with built-in role governance (Leader and Co-Leader controls), multi-format export (.docx, .pdf, .txt), and completely private personal spaces within the same unified dashboard.

## Operating Context
Accessed on desktop and mobile web viewports. Used during live team syncs, class study groups, project planning sessions, and individual quiet drafting.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Express.js backend with PostgreSQL (Neon) and framework-free Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, ES6 JavaScript.
- Team workspace roles: Leader, Co-Leader, Member with explicit leadership request workflows.
- Rich-text notebook editor with strict tab isolation ([Home], [Insert], [Draw], [Layout]), dynamic table generator, flowchart shapes, and export engines.
- Personal Space isolated with `is_private = TRUE` ensuring complete confidentiality.
- Theme-harmonized Light Mode and Dark Mode with full keyboard and mobile navigation drawer support.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: GroupSpace
- Modern, clean, and high-contrast typography without gimmicks or distracting AI visual tropes.
- Responsive accessibility with WCAG AA compliance (≥ 4.5:1 text contrast) and minimum 11px text floor.

## Product Principles
1. **Immediate Clarity**: Clean visual hierarchy and intuitive scanability across all devices.
2. **Strict Privacy Boundaries**: Full collaboration in shared spaces; complete isolation in personal spaces.
3. **Harmonious & Accessible Craft**: High-contrast typography, natural elevation, and predictable interactive states.
4. **Lightweight Reliability**: Zero heavy frameworks; fast, snappy, and lightweight DOM operations.
